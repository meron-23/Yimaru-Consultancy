================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF TÜRKIYE — TÜRKIYE BURSLARI SCHOLARSHIPS 2026/2027
Official Call for International Higher Education Studies in Turkey
================================================================================

1. SCHOLARSHIP OVERVIEW
Türkiye Scholarships is a government-funded, competitive scholarship program awarded to outstanding students and researchers from across the globe to pursue full-time or short-term programs at top universities in the Republic of Türkiye. Ethiopian students have historically formed a vital part of this international alumni community.

2. DEGREE LEVELS & ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Degree Levels:
  * Undergraduate (Bachelor): Candidates must be under 21 years of age, with minimum academic GPA of 75% in secondary school graduation / entrance examinations.
  * Master’s Degree: Candidates must be under 30 years of age, with minimum academic GPA of 75% in Bachelor degree studies.
  * PhD (Doctorate): Candidates must be under 35 years of age, with minimum academic GPA of 80% in Master degree studies.
- Eligible Nationalities: Open to all international applicants, including Ethiopian citizens.
- Health: Candidates must be medically fit to pursue studies abroad.

3. SCHOLARSHIP FUNDING & COVERAGE PROVISIONS
The scholarship provides comprehensive full funding throughout the duration of the degree:
- Full Tuition Fee coverage across all enrolled universities.
- One-Year Turkish Language Course (TÖMER) provided free of charge prior to academic studies.
- Free Accommodation in modern state university dormitories (including meal cards).
- Monthly Living Allowance (Stipend):
  * Undergraduate: 3,500 TL per month
  * Master's Degree: 5,000 TL per month
  * PhD Degree: 6,500 TL per month
- One-time return flight ticket (Ethiopia – Turkey – Ethiopia).
- Comprehensive public health insurance coverage during the entire stay.

4. APPLICATION WINDOW & TIMELINE
- Application Period: January 10 to February 20 annually.
- Interview Invitations: April to June. Interviews are held in person at the Turkish Embassy in Addis Ababa or virtually.
- Final Results Announcement: Late July to early August.
- Arrival and Academic Orientation: Mid-September.

5. TOP TURKISH UNIVERSITIES
- Middle East Technical University (METU / ODTÜ), Ankara
- Boğaziçi University, Istanbul
- Istanbul Technical University (İTÜ), Istanbul
- Koç University, Istanbul
- Bilkent University, Ankara
- Hacettepe University, Ankara
- Ege University, Izmir

6. STRATEGIC ADVISOR TIP FOR ETHIOPIAN APPLICANTS
When listing your university preferences on the application portal, avoid selecting only universities located in central Istanbul. Allocating choices to premier institutions in Ankara (such as METU or Hacettepe) or Izmir (Ege University) dramatically increases your likelihood of being shortlisted for the interview round.
