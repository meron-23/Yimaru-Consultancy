================================================================================
GOVERNMENT OF HUNGARY: STIPENDIUM HUNGARICUM SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAMME 2026/2027
Official Call for Applications — International Higher Education Studies in Hungary
================================================================================

1. PROGRAMME OVERVIEW
The Stipendium Hungaricum Scholarship Programme was launched in 2013 by the Hungarian Government. The core mission of the programme is to increase the number of international students in Hungary and encourage Hungarian higher education institutions to attract top international talent. Today, thousands of international students from over 90 partner countries, including Ethiopia, pursue higher education in Hungary annually.

2. ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA & APPLICANT PROFILE
Eligible applicants must satisfy the following conditions:
- Citizenship: Citizen of an eligible partner country (Ethiopian nationals are fully eligible through bilateral educational agreements).
- Degree Levels Offered: Bachelor (BA/BSc), Master (MA/MSc), One-tier master (OTM), and Doctoral (PhD/DLA) degree programs.
- Minimum Academic Qualifications:
  * For Bachelor studies: High school graduation certificate with minimum 75% average (or Ethiopian University Entrance Exam completion with distinction).
  * For Master studies: Bachelor degree with minimum CGPA of 3.0 / 4.0 (or equivalent European Good/Very Good standing).
  * For Doctoral studies: Master degree with strong research proposal and supervisor pre-approval.
- Language Proficiency: B2/C1 English level. Accepted tests: IELTS 6.0+, TOEFL iBT 72+, or Duolingo English Test 105+.
- Age Limit: Applicants must have reached 18 years of age by August 31, 2026.

3. SCHOLARSHIP COVERAGE & FINANCIAL PROVISIONS
The scholarship provides full funding throughout the entire duration of studies:
- Tuition Fees: 100% full tuition waiver across all participating Hungarian state universities.
- Monthly Living Stipend:
  * Bachelor and Master levels: HUF 43,700 (approx. €120–140) per month until completion of studies.
  * Doctoral level: HUF 140,000 per month for the first phase (4 semesters), increasing to HUF 180,000 per month for the second phase.
- Accommodation Support: Free dormitory placement on university campus OR a monthly housing contribution of HUF 40,000 towards private rental costs.
- Medical Insurance: Full national health insurance services according to Hungarian law, plus supplementary private health insurance up to HUF 65,000 per year.
- Round-trip flight ticket is covered by the Ethiopian Ministry of Education for nominated candidates.

4. APPLICATION WINDOW & DEADLINES
- Application System Opens: November 15, 2025
- Application Deadline: January 15, 2026 (2:00 PM Central European Time)
- Institutional Entrance Examinations & Interviews: March to May 2026
- Final Selection Results: End of June 2026
- Academic Year Commences: September 2026

5. POPULAR FIELDS OF STUDY
- Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence
- Mechanical, Civil, and Electrical Engineering
- Agricultural Sciences & Water Management
- International Relations & Economics
- Public Health & Biotechnology

6. STRATEGIC ADVISOR NOTE FOR ETHIOPIAN APPLICANTS
This is a dual-application process. Candidates must submit an application on the official DreamApply portal and must also submit a nomination dossier to the Ethiopian Ministry of Education (FDRE MoE). Applying to regional universities with specialized engineering faculties (such as University of Debrecen, University of Szeged, or University of Pécs) yields a significantly higher acceptance rate compared to institutions solely located in central Budapest.
